Private listening / iPhone alpha

Albums worth remembering.

Alcove LP is a quiet, album-first home for Apple Music. Save the records that matter, queue whole albums, and keep the notes and listening history on your device.

Join the free TestFlight alpha ↗ See what is inside

Requires iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Music catalog playback requires an active Apple Music subscription. The TestFlight alpha is free; public version 1.0 is planned as a one-time $14.99 paid download and is not yet available.

No feed.
Just records.

The whole record. Not the feed.

Alcove LP starts with albums, keeps their order intact, and makes room for the memory around the music. The first alpha is deliberately small and useful.

A place for the music you keep

Your records. Your shelf.

Search is for finding. The shelf is for returning. Save an album, leave a note, and come back without turning your listening into content for somebody else.

The TestFlight alpha is free, has no ads, and contains no purchases. The planned $14.99 public version is still in development.

Listening is not telemetry.

Alcove LP has no account system or Alcove server for the app to call. Its source policy rejects analytics, tracking, crash-reporting SDKs, CloudKit, and general-purpose networking.

No accountYour Apple ID password is never provided to Alcove LP.
No analyticsNo product analytics, tracking, ads, or behavioral telemetry.
Local by defaultShelf, queue, notes, and listening history stay on your device.
You can clear itReset an album's history or clear all local history from the app.

One mode in alpha. Three required for version 1.0.

Modes change listening behavior, not the sound. LP is available in the current TestFlight alpha. Cassette and 8-track are required for public version 1.0, but they are not available yet.

Available in alpha

LP

Linear whole-album playback, no shuffle, and a deliberate end.

Required for public 1.0

Cassette

A continuous album timeline with the character of a counter, without invented metadata.

Required for public 1.0

8-track

A circular album session that can return from the last track to the first.
