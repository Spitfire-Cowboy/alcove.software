Before your first listen.

A few things to know about the app, the music, and what you keep.

What does $14.99 include?

The one-time US App Store price gives your records, notes, whole-album queue, and listening history one private place on your iPhone. All Alcove LP features are included, with no Alcove subscription or in-app purchase.

Do I need Apple Music?

An active Apple Music subscription is required for catalog access and catalog playback. Albums already in your Music library can be browsed and queued without a subscription when Apple makes them playable on the device. Music is not included in the app price.

What can I listen to?

Version 1.0 supports Apple Music and playable albums in your Music library on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. LP mode plays albums in track order without shuffle. Files imports, Spotify, TIDAL, cassette, and 8-track modes are not included.

Where do my notes and history go?

They stay on your device. Alcove LP has no account, sync service, or developer listening telemetry. You can clear your listening history in the app. Apple device backups remain controlled by Apple and your device settings.
