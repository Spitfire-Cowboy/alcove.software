Before your first listen.

A few things to know about the app, the music, and what you keep.

What does $14.99 include?

The planned one-time US App Store price includes all Alcove LP features: your album shelf, whole-album queue, private notes, and local listening history. There is no Alcove subscription or in-app purchase. Apple has approved version 1.0; purchase availability is still pending.

Do I need Apple Music?

Yes, an active Apple Music subscription is required to play Apple Music catalog albums. The app price does not include that subscription or music. You can explore the shelf and notes with the built-in demo albums before connecting.

What can I listen to?

Version 1.0 supports Apple Music on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. LP mode plays albums in track order without shuffle. Files imports, downloaded-library browsing, Spotify, TIDAL, cassette, and 8-track modes are not included.

Where do my notes and history go?

They stay on your device. Alcove LP has no account, sync service, or developer listening telemetry. You can clear your listening history in the app. Apple device backups remain controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Can I try it before launch?

The prerelease beta is free through TestFlight, subject to beta availability. It is separate from the upcoming paid App Store app; it is not a paid-app trial or a purchase.
