Help / Version 1.0

Alcove LP support

Email john@spitfirecowboy.com for product support. TestFlight users can also use Send Beta Feedback on Alcove LP's page in the TestFlight app.

Before reporting playback: confirm that Apple Music plays normally on the same iPhone and that your Apple Music subscription is active.

Connecting Apple Music

Tap Connect Apple Music in Alcove LP and allow access when iOS asks. If access was previously denied, open iPhone Settings, find Alcove LP, and allow Media & Apple Music access before trying again.

Catalog search or playback is unavailable

Apple Music catalog access and full-track playback depend on Apple's service, your region, network connection, and an active subscription. The two built-in demo albums remain available without connecting Apple Music.

Your shelf, queue, notes, and history

These stay on your device. Alcove LP provides no Alcove account, sync service, or Alcove-operated cloud backup. Apple device backups remain controlled by Apple and your device settings. History can be reset per album or cleared from the History tab. Deleting the app removes local Alcove LP data, subject to that Apple device-backup behavior.

What to include with feedback

  • What you were trying to do.
  • The album or screen involved.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, when it does not contain anything you want to keep private.

Do not include your Apple ID password, payment information, or private listening notes.

Current scope

Version 1.0 is for iPhone, Apple Music, and LP mode only. Spotify, TIDAL, cassette mode, 8-track mode, desktop, and Apple TV are not part of this release.

Build 12 has been submitted to App Review as a $14.99 one-time paid download. It is not yet available to purchase. The prerelease remains free through TestFlight while review is pending.
